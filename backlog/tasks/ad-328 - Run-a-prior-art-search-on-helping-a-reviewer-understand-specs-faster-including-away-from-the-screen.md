Reviewing an LLD is the slowest stop in the LID walk for the developer. A trial on 2026-11-18 turned the task-tracker LLD into a 25-minute listening script (chapters, worked numbers, points to weigh, recaps, pauses and chimes) read by a phone's voice: much better than reading the document aloud, but still too dense to follow while doing something else. Run the prior-art-search skill on ways to speed up or deepen a reviewer's understanding of a design spec: summaries ordered for review, guided walkthroughs, question-led or quiz-style review, diagrams, change-focused views, conversational audio (two-host podcast generators), neural text-to-speech with prosody control, and published research on comprehension of technical documents by ear and on the page. Build on docs/prior-art/2026-10-27-review-and-comprehension-burden.md and docs/prior-art/2026-10-30-change-presentation.md rather than repeating them.
