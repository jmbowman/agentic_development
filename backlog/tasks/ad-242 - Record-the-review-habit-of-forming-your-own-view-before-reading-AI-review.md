## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Add to the review guidance (AGENTS.md or review-wave instructions) the rule that a reviewer forms their own view before reading any AI review or AI explanation of tests. AI review comments anchor reviewers on what they flag and saved no time (Tufano et al. 2025); low-quality test explanations lowered accuracy while raising confidence (Kaufman et al. 2026). Done when the rule is written where reviewers and review subagents see it.

**Why it matters:** Counters anchoring in human review of agent work.
